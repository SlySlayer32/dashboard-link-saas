# Vision

## The Why

Frontline and field workers represent 80%+ of the global workforce but are the most underserved when it comes to communication tools. Field workers in industries like cleaning, construction, healthcare, and hospitality face a daily information crisis: their job-critical information—schedules, client addresses, door codes, task instructions—is scattered across WhatsApp threads, SMS chains, and multiple apps. Nothing is organized. Nothing is reliable.

**The root cause is not laziness or bad communication—it's the absence of a single, always-current source of truth that workers can access without friction.**

Existing solutions fail because:
- **Workforce platforms** (Connecteam, Deputy) require app downloads that 60% of workers never complete
- **Team chat apps** (Slack, Teams) bury information in chat history with no structure
- **WhatsApp/SMS** are simple but terrible at organizing; updates require manual resending

**No tool exists that delivers structured, real-time daily information to field workers with zero friction—and proves to managers that workers actually received it.**

## North Star

**Give every field worker a single, always-current dashboard they can access in one tap—with zero app installs, zero logins, and zero confusion.**

Success means:
- Managers stop being the information bottleneck
- Workers start every day with clarity instead of calling to ask "What's the code?"
- Updates reach workers instantly without manual resending
- Casual and part-time staff get the same reliable information on Day 1

## Principles

**1. Simple Over Feature-Rich**  
Competitors are building "workforce management platforms." We're building "a daily info delivery system." That focus is the moat. Every feature must answer: "Does this help workers see today's information faster?"

**2. Zero Friction for Workers**  
No app, no login, no account. If it adds a step for workers, it's rejected. Casual and rotating staff must work perfectly on Day 1.

**3. Works With Existing Tools**  
Connect to Google Calendar, Airtable, Notion—no workflow rebuild. Adoption collapses when managers must change habits.

**4. One SMS Per Day Discipline**  
Single daily notification prevents notification fatigue. Competitors spam workers who mute everything, then miss critical updates.

**5. Referral-First Growth**  
Workers often work 2-3 part-time jobs. Every dashboard they see is passive brand exposure to other managers. Two-sided referral incentives (reward referrer + discount new org) improve customer quality: referred customers have 37% higher retention (Deloitte).
